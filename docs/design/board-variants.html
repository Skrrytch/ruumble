Board in the room: three variants

Each row shows the same section: rooms with 0, 1, 3 and 12 posts, and on the right your own room (light blue) with 2 posts. The graphic replaces the counter. It only hints at the amount (1, 2, or 3 or more notes); exact numbers are in the sidebar. Empty rooms show nothing, except your own room: there an empty wall invites you to pin something.

A · Cork board on the wall

A small framed board hangs on the wall at the top right. White and light blue notes are held by a pin. New token --color-cork (warm beige), otherwise only colours from the tokens. The most clearly recognised as a “board”.

B · Notes directly on the wall

No board, just slightly tilted notes with coloured pins, as if pinned to an office wall. Very light, fits the reduced floor plan best. Only colours from the tokens.

C · Chalkboard with magnets

A slim dark blue framed board with small cards held by round magnets. Looks more “technical” and calmer, and echoes the elevator panel. Only colours from the tokens.
